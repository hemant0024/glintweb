The problem

Matches rarely die from bad photos. They die in the middle of a conversation, on a reply that took twenty minutes to write and said nothing.

Glint is for that reply. Not a chatbot answering as a stranger, and not a canned line off a list. Three drafts built from the thread you are actually in, in the voice you actually use.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What is Glint?

Glint is an AI wingman for dating apps. You paste a chat from Hinge, Tinder or Bumble and get three reply drafts, one in each voice (Genuine, Glint, Roast), to copy back into the app.

Is Glint free?

Yes. The free tier gives 5 reply generations a day across all three voices. Glint Pro adds unlimited replies, an opener generator and a profile audit for $6.99 a month at launch.

Which dating apps does Glint support?

Glint works with screenshots or pasted text from Hinge, Tinder, Bumble and every other major dating app. The input is the conversation itself rather than an integration, so it works anywhere you match.

How is Glint different from a general AI chatbot?

A general chatbot gives you one reply that often reads as robotic. Glint gives three, each in a different voice, so you can compare and pick the phrasing closest to how you actually talk, then edit it.

Does Glint store my conversations or train AI on them?

No. Screenshots and chat text are processed only long enough to generate the replies, then discarded. Glint does not train models on user conversations and does not sell user data.

Is Glint on iOS and Android?

Both apps are in build. Join the waitlist and you get the download link the day it goes live.

Will they know I used Glint?

Not unless you tell them. Glint drafts in your voice, and you pick the one that sounds like you, edit anything that doesn't, and send it from your own account.
