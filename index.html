FAQ

Questions people ask

What is Glint?

Glint is an AI dating-app wingman. You paste a chat from Hinge, Tinder, or Bumble, pick a tone — Genuine, Rizz, or Roast — and get three reply candidates to copy back into the dating app.

Is Glint free?

Yes. The free tier gives 5 reply generations per day across all three tones. Glint Pro adds unlimited replies, an opener generator, and a profile audit feature for $6.99 / month at launch.

Which dating apps does Glint support?

Glint works with screenshots or pasted text from Hinge, Tinder, Bumble, and other major dating apps. The input is the conversation itself, not an integration, so it works with any dating platform.

How is Glint different from a generic AI chatbot?

Generic chatbots produce one reply that often sounds robotic. Glint generates three options on different tones (warm, charming, playful) so you can compare and pick the phrasing closest to how you actually talk, then edit.

Does Glint store my conversations or train AI on them?

No. Screenshots and chat text are processed only long enough to generate the replies, then discarded. Glint does not train AI models on user conversations and does not sell user data.

Is Glint available on iOS and Android?

Glint is pre-launch. iOS and Android apps will ship after the waitlist build. Sign up below for early access.

What does “rizz” mean in the context of Glint?

Rizz is slang for charisma — the ability to charm or flirt in conversation. In Glint, “Rizz” is one of three tone settings: confident and witty, between the warm “Genuine” tone and the playful “Roast” tone. More on what rizz means.
