What Is Rizz? A Guide to Charm on Dating Apps

Oxford made “rizz” its 2023 Word of the Year, which is mostly just the internet noticing what teenagers had already named: charisma in conversation. Below: what good rizz actually looks like, why most attempts at it fail, and how to apply the idea to your Hinge and Tinder replies without sounding like you’re trying.

What rizz means (the short version)

Rizz is shortened from “charisma.” In its narrow sense, it’s your ability to charm or flirt in a conversation — verbal style, timing, the way you make someone feel briefly interesting. “W rizz” means good rizz, “L rizz” means bad. The term spread out from TikTok in 2022 and now mostly lives in dating-app discourse.

Originally the word started as Bronx slang, popularized by streamer Kai Cenat. The flat dictionary definition: a person’s style, charm, or attractiveness — their ability to attract a romantic partner.

The three components of rizz that matter

1. Specificity

Rizz isn’t a magic line. It’s the move of paying attention to one specific detail and reacting to it. Everyone has experienced the difference between “you’re really cool” (no rizz) and “the way you described that show was unhinged in a way I respect” (rizz). The second one proves you were listening.

2. Brevity

People with rizz say less. A funny line ruined by being explained loses all its rizz. The cadence is short — sentences you could fit on a sticky note. The longer you talk, the less mysterious you are.

3. Confidence about a small claim

Rizz needs a point of view. Not “I’m great” (which is unverifiable), but tiny opinionated claims — “hot dogs are sandwiches”, “every airport bar is the same airport bar’s spiritual cousin.” Confident on small things signals you might be confident on larger things.

W rizz vs L rizz, side by side

THEM
just got back from a road trip up the coast 🌊
L RIZZ
that sounds so cool!! I love road trips. how was it??

Nothing to grab onto. Compliments the trip vaguely; bounces a generic question back.

W RIZZ
coastal road trips have a 70% gas station snack quality problem. did you suffer or did you have a system?

Specific premise. Mildly contrarian. Asks them to defend something tiny.

How to develop rizz (without trying to be a different person)

  1. Notice the smallest detail. Before you respond, look at their profile or last message and find the most specific thing in it. Build the reply around that.
  2. Cut your first draft in half. Whatever you write, delete the front of it. The setup is usually the boring part. Start with the punchy bit.
  3. Have one weird strong opinion you’re willing to deploy. A take on coffee, airports, IKEA furniture names — anything. Use it once when relevant. People with rizz have a small library of these.
  4. Don’t explain the joke. If they don’t get it, that’s information. Don’t backpedal.
  5. Reply slightly less often than they reply to you. Not as a game — as breathing room. Constant immediate replies kill rizz.

The AI question: does AI give you rizz?

Short answer: AI can do the phrasing part, not the noticing part. The component of rizz that’s pure verbal flair — the kind of thing AI is good at — is maybe a third of the equation. The other two thirds (paying attention, having a take) have to come from you.

FAQ

What does “unspoken rizz” mean?

Charm that doesn’t require words — physicality, timing, the way someone holds attention. The meme version is “walking past someone slowly without saying anything.”

Is “rizz” only for men?

No. It started as masculine-coded but the term applies to anyone in a conversation.

Can a pickup line have rizz?

Only when the user signals self-awareness about it. Sincere pickup lines are L rizz by default. Pickup line as a deliberate joke can be W rizz if delivered with confidence and brevity.
